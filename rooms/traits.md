# Room: Traits

**Live page:** `Soulflake.html#traits`
**Lead:** How you move. Not a type.

Likert: No / A little / Often / That’s me (1–4)

- open — Strange art, strange food, strange ideas feel like oxygen.
- extra — A room with people in it gives me energy more often than it costs me.
- cons — I finish the last 10% even when the idea is already bored of me.
- agree — I give people the benefit of the doubt, even when it costs me.
- neuro — Small slights stay in my body longer than I want them to.
