# Room: Conflict

**Live page:** `Soulflake.html` arm `conflict`
**Lead:** What you do when it heats up. One scene at a time.

## Live wording (short)

- late — They’re late again. Name the pattern / Go quiet / Joke it off
- heat — The room is heating up. Stay and say the hard line / Leave and cool off / Soften so nobody explodes
- repair — The next morning. Text first and own your part / Wait for them / Act like nothing happened

## 09-08 wording to prefer

- late — They’re late again with a thin apology. Your first move: Name the pattern / Go quiet / Joke it off, bring it up later
- heat — The room is heating up. You: Stay in it and say the hard line / Leave and cool off / Soften so nobody explodes
- repair — The next morning, you: Text first and own your part / Wait for them / Act like nothing happened until they do

The late option must keep “bring it up later.” Joke-only reads as dismissal.
