The hole

The internet isolates people. Quizzes type you and leave you there. “Sounds like me” is not useful.

What this is

A living profile that gets more useful the more you tell it. Six bands: Traits, Taste, Bond, Values, Conflict, Context. Match, compare, rooms, scripts. Not a type quiz. Not astrology.

Why it matters

Know yourself clearly enough to act. Connect to people and nights that actually fit. Success test: that’s uncomfortably specific — and useful.

How it works

Start with questions you like answering. The flake stays visible. Click an arm to carve it. You are never done. More truth in means better matches, rooms, Tuesday sentences. Empty arms stay frost. Lopsided is honest.
